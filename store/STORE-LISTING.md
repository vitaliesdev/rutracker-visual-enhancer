## English (`en`) — primary locale

**Name**

RuTracker Visual Enhancer - Posters, Filters & Dark Mode

**Short description**

Transform RuTracker with posters, quick filters, infinite scrolling, and a polished Light or Dark interface.

**Detailed description**

RuTracker Visual Enhancer makes supported tracker result pages faster to scan, easier to navigate, and more comfortable to use.

It adds a visual poster column, compact topic summaries, availability badges, clearer author and date cards, and direct access to the original .torrent and Magnet links already provided by the tracker. A lightweight toolbar lets you filter loaded results instantly, show only seeded items, choose visible columns, change poster size, and switch between Light and Dark themes.

Optional infinite scrolling loads the next result page as you approach the bottom while preserving filters and your navigation position. The advanced search panel can be collapsed to leave more room for results.

Key features:
• Posters and short summaries loaded only near the viewport
• Fast local filtering and seeded-only view
• Optional infinite scrolling with clear page separators
• Configurable columns and three poster sizes
• Full-site Light and Dark themes
• Original .torrent and Magnet actions
• Localized interface in English, Ukrainian, Russian, Romanian, and Spanish
• Also shows posters on compatible trackers you enable from the extension icon
• No ads, analytics, accounts, remote executable code, or external API

Preferences and a limited topic cache stay in the browser. The extension does not send browsing data to its developer or sell data. Topic information is requested directly from the tracker currently open in the browser.

This is an independent, unofficial extension and is not affiliated with RuTracker or any supported website. Users are responsible for following applicable laws and the terms of the websites they visit.
